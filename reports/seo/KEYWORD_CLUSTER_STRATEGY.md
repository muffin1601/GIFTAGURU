# Keyword cluster strategy

The source inventory contains **891 entries representing 731 distinct phrases**. The 160 entries on the 3–6 Piece Keywords tab repeat exact rows from the main tab and remain as separate tracking rows in the master CSV. Strategy is consolidated by intent, not by deleting source entries.

## Ownership model

- Homepage: broad brand/category positioning and navigation, not every corporate-gifting variation.
- /corporate-gifting: primary commercial solution page and priority destination for broad non-brand solution queries observed in GSC.
- Category pages: joining, premium, luxury and eco browse intent.
- Use-case pages: onboarding, appreciation, client, event, leadership, partner and bulk programmes.
- Gift-set pages: consolidate all 3–6-piece permutations into four strong pages.
- Industry pages: only where a distinct use case and useful offering exist. Other industry phrases remain consolidated; no thin city/industry doorway pages.
- Blog: informational planning, budgeting, branding and comparison queries.

## Priority sequence

1. Protect canonical/indexation hygiene and deploy the broken-product fix.
2. Improve '/corporate-gifting', '/categories/luxury-gifts', '/categories/premium-gifts', '/gifting/events-conferences' and the budget guide using observed GSC intent.
3. Build contextual links from relevant products/articles; do not add repetitive sitewide keyword anchors.
4. Re-measure after crawling. Use query-by-page data before declaring cannibalization.
5. Create new pages only after proving unique intent, offer support and content differentiation.

Search volumes and difficulty are intentionally absent: the supplied files do not contain them, and GSC impressions are not search volume.
